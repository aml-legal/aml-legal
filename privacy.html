01

No accounts, no email, nothing to remember

Paste an address, get a score. That's the whole flow. No name, no email, no password to forget six months from now. Nothing about you is required to use any part of this tool.

02

The one thing you give us: a wallet address

An address is already public. Anyone can look up its balance and full history without asking you anything — that's how blockchains work. So checking one isn't the privacy-sensitive part of crypto.

What matters more: we don't try to figure out who's behind it, we don't log it against your IP, and you never connect a wallet or sign anything to run a check. The address goes into the scoring method on the homepage and that's the extent of it.

The honest caveat

Right now, nothing you paste leaves your browser to produce a result — the whole scan runs client-side. If that ever changes and a real screening API sits behind this tool, this page gets rewritten first, not after.

03

Fonts, a live chat widget, and nothing else

Nothing here builds an ad profile or follows you around the web after you leave. Two things on this site do set cookies or talk to a third party, and both are named here rather than buried:

  • Google Fonts — loads the typefaces you're reading right now. Google's request, not ours.
  • Smartsupp (live chat widget) — shows a consent prompt of its own before setting anything; if you accept, it sets a cookie to keep your conversation attached to you across the visit and stores the transcript along with your IP and browser info on their servers so a human on our side can actually reply. See Smartsupp's own privacy policy for what they do with that data on their end.

If we ever add tracking of our own beyond what's named above, this page says so first, with a way to opt out.

04

Contact form and live chat

Two ways to reach us, both go to a human, not a mailing list or "our partners":

  • The contact form is handled by Formspree, a third-party form-processing service — whatever you type goes through their servers and lands directly in our inbox. Formspree does not get added as a marketing contact anywhere; see Formspree's privacy policy for how they handle submissions in transit.
  • The live chat (bottom-right bubble) is answered by our team during working hours; outside those hours it takes a message the same way the form does. See the cookies section above for what Smartsupp stores to make that possible.
  • You can also just email [email protected] directly, skipping both of the above.
05

The list of things we never touch

  • No KYC. We're not an exchange, and we're not going to start acting like one.
  • No IP address logged against a specific check.
  • No device fingerprinting, ever, for any reason.
  • Nobody buys this data, because nobody's selling it.
06

If any of this changes

The date at the top moves and the change gets written down here — not buried in a version-history page nobody reads.
